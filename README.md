# QBI
Pairwise quantile spectra cannot determine triple dependence, while ordinary bispectra can vanish through cancellation. We construct a rank-based quantile bispectrum using centered threshold indicators at distinct times. It vanishes under independence, is invariant under strictly increasing marginal transformations, and requires no moments of the observations. Stationary examples separate third-order from pairwise information and show when mixed quantile levels are necessary. Under geometric absolute regularity and local smoothness, we derive a Gaussian limit for fixed rank moments with marginal corrections, prove uniform consistency of a lag-window estimator, and justify fixed-resolution Gaussian multiplier tests. Simulations demonstrate these identification differences and reduced sensitivity to the specified contamination. Lagged marginal corrections improve median-test calibration under strong dependence, although small-sample overrejection remains. Bearing vibration and nearshore-current applications show rejections across several quantile levels in windows where median tests do not reject.
